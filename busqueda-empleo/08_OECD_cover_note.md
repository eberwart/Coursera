I am applying to the Economics Department pooled campaign for Economists (PAL6). I am a Chilean national, PhD in Finance (University of Manchester), with more than twenty years in a central bank and financial regulator and a research agenda in household finance and behavioral public policy.

My recent paper, *Search and Negotiation with Biased Beliefs in Consumer Credit Markets* (NBER Working Paper 35605, with Higgins, Kulkarni and Truffa), is a randomized controlled trial with 112,063 loan seekers, using the regulator’s administrative data. I can contribute immediately to country surveys and cross-country work on financial markets, household behavior, inclusion and evidence-based policy, and I am prepared to relocate to Paris.
